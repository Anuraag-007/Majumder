Part 1

Masters: the fixed lists

Masters are the lists the ERP uses everywhere: your materials, your customers and suppliers, your machines. Fill them in once; after that every screen lets you pick from a list instead of typing, and rates, GST and labour fill in automatically.

Fill them in this order (each one uses the ones before it)

  1. Units: already filled (Kg, Nos, Roll…). Add only if you need a new one.
  2. Locations: the 4 main places are already there.
  3. Items: all your materials and products.
  4. Parties: suppliers and customers.
  5. Machines: knitting and dyeing machines.
  6. Processes: labour rates (3 are already there; set your real rates).
  7. BOM / Recipes: dye recipes and packing standards.
  8. Party Price List (under Sales): special prices for customers.
Who can fill Masters: Admin, Management, Purchase, Sales and Data Entry logins. Factory logins (Knitting, Dyeing, Finishing, Store) can see the lists but cannot change them.

When a price changes, add a new line with the new date; the ERP always uses the latest. Customers without a special price get the item's standard rate.

Good to know

  • No duplicates. Two items, parties or machines cannot have the same name; the ERP tells you if one exists.
  • Used records cannot be deleted. Once an item or party is used in an entry it is protected. You can still edit it (spelling, phone number…).
  • Changes apply going forward. A new rate, GST % or labour rate affects new entries; saved entries keep their values.
  • Search and sort: every list has a search box; click a column heading to sort.
Part 2

From yarn purchase to finished rolls

One real example all the way through: you buy 1,000 kg of Cotton Yarn 30s, knit 600 kg of it, dye it Red, and end up with 560 kg / 24 rolls of finished fabric.

The material moves through four places, one entry at a time

Main Storeyarn, dyes, packing
Knitting Factoryyarn → grey fabric
Color Factorygrey → dyed fabric
Finished Goods Storefinished rolls
#StepWho does itWhat happens
1Purchase OrderPurchaseYou order yarn from the supplier
2Goods Receipt (GRN)StoreYarn arrives at the Main Store and gets its lot number (L-0001)
3Purchase BillPurchase / AccountsSupplier's bill recorded (money only)
4Yarn Issue to KnittingStoreYarn sent to the Knitting Factory
5KnittingKnittingYarn becomes grey fabric with the same lot number, and goes straight to the Color Factory
6DyeingDyeingGrey becomes coloured fabric
7Finishing & PackingFinishingColoured becomes finished rolls in the Finished Goods Store
Golden rule: each step can only use what the previous step put there. If a list says "no stock here", the step before it has not been done yet.
One lot number, start to finish: the ERP gives the lot number once, on the GRN (L-0001, L-0002 …). Nobody types it. Yarn issue, knitting, dyeing, finishing and the challan all pick that same number from a list, so lot L-0001 is the same lot from the supplier's yarn to the customer's invoice.
Before you start: the items in the example must exist in Masters → Items (Cotton Yarn 30s = Yarn, Grey Single Jersey 30s = Grey Fabric, Dyed Single Jersey 30s = Colored Fabric, Finished Single Jersey 30s = Finished Goods, plus your dyes, chemicals and packing). Add the supplier in Masters → Parties.

Part 3

Results, selling and quick fixes

Where to see what the lot cost and where it is, how to sell it, and what to do when the ERP shows a message.

AWhat the ERP shows you at the end

Where to lookWhat you see
Inventory → Stock Summary (Finished Goods Store)560 kg / 24 rolls, lot L-0001, with its value
Costing → Lot Costing SheetThe exact cost of lot L-0001: yarn + dyes + packing + all labour + overheads + wastage = cost per kg, per roll, per meter
Production → Batch / Lot TrackingLot L-0001 from the supplier's GRN (1,000 kg received) → 600 kg yarn knitted → 585 grey → 570 dyed → 560 finished = 93.3% yield, then kg sold and kg left
Wastage → Wastage ReportKnitting 15 kg, dyeing 15 kg, finishing 10 kg, with their value
For accurate costing also enter each month's power bill, rent, maintenance and similar costs in Costing → Overhead Entries. The ERP spreads them over the kilograms finished that month.

#ScreenWhat to do
