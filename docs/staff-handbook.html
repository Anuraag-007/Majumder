Part 1

Masters: the fixed lists

Masters are the lists the ERP uses everywhere: your materials, your suppliers and customers, and the agencies that knit, dye and finish for you. Fill them in once; after that every screen lets you pick from a list instead of typing, and rates, GST and job charges fill in automatically.

Fill them in this order (each one uses the ones before it)

  1. Units: already filled (Kg, Nos, Roll…). Add only if you need a new one.
  2. Locations: the stores are already there; each agency gets its own when you add it as a party.
  3. Items: all your materials and products.
  4. Parties: suppliers, customers and job-work agencies (knitting, dyeing, finishing).
  5. Machines: optional; knitting and dyeing machines.
  6. Processes: standard rates per kg (3 are already there), used when an agency has no rate of its own.
  7. BOM / Recipes: dye recipes and packing standards.
  8. Party Price List (under Sales): special prices for customers.
Who can fill Masters: Admin, Management, Purchase, Sales and Data Entry logins. Factory logins (Knitting, Dyeing, Finishing, Store) can see the lists but cannot change them.

6Processes: standard rates

A standard rate per kg for each stage. Each agency's own rate (on its party record) comes first; this one is used when an agency has none, and for work in your own factory. The ERP uses the rate to calculate job charges automatically, and they go into your cost per kg. Already there: Knitting ₹8/kg, Dyeing ₹15/kg, Finishing & Packing ₹6/kg.

Good to know

  • No duplicates. Two items, parties or machines cannot have the same name; the ERP tells you if one exists.
  • Used records cannot be deleted. Once an item or party is used in an entry it is protected. You can still edit it (spelling, phone number…).
  • Changes apply going forward. A new rate, GST % or agency rate affects new entries; saved entries keep their values.
  • Search and sort: every list has a search box; click a column heading to sort.
Part 2

From yarn purchase to finished rolls

One real example all the way through: you buy 1,000 kg of Cotton Yarn 30s, send 600 kg to the knitting agency Maa Tara Knitting, have it dyed Red and finished by Bengal Dyeing House, and end up with 560 kg / 24 rolls of finished fabric.

The material moves through your store and the agencies, one entry at a time

Main Storeyarn, dyes, packing
Knitting agencyyarn → grey fabric
Dyeing / finishing agencygrey → dyed → finished
Finished Goods Storefinished rolls
#StepWho does itWhat happens
1Purchase OrderPurchaseYou order yarn from the supplier
2Goods Receipt (GRN)StoreYarn arrives at the Main Store and gets its lot number (L-0001)
3Purchase BillPurchase / AccountsSupplier's bill recorded (money only)
4Yarn Issue to KnittingStoreYarn sent to the knitting agency with a job work challan
5KnittingKnittingThe agency's grey fabric is recorded with the same lot number, and goes straight to the dyeing agency
6DyeingDyeingThe dyeing agency turns grey into coloured fabric
7Finishing & PackingFinishingThe finishing agency's finished rolls come into the Finished Goods Store
Golden rule: each step can only use what the previous step put there, at that agency. If a list says "no stock here", the step before it has not been done yet, or the material was sent to another agency.
One lot number, start to finish: the ERP gives the lot number once, on the GRN (L-0001, L-0002 …). It fills in by itself; type your own only if you want a different number (for example the supplier's lot). Yarn issue, knitting, dyeing, finishing and the challan all pick that same number from a list, so lot L-0001 is the same lot from the supplier's yarn to the customer's invoice.
Before you start: the items in the example must exist in Masters → Items (Cotton Yarn 30s = Yarn, Grey Single Jersey 30s = Grey Fabric, Dyed Single Jersey 30s = Colored Fabric, Finished Single Jersey 30s = Finished Goods, plus your dyes, chemicals and packing). Add the supplier and both agencies in Masters → Parties: Maa Tara Knitting (Job Worker, tick Does knitting, rate 9) and Bengal Dyeing House (Job Worker, tick Does dyeing rate 15 and Does finishing rate 6).

CPaying the agencies
